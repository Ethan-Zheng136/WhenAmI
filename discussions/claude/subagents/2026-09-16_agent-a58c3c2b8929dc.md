## 二、LeJEPA 的可辨识性理论 — 关键澄清

我通读了 2511.08544 的 §4。**这里有一个容易被误读的点，对你的选题直接相关**：

LeJEPA 的 "identifiability" **不是隐变量可辨识性（latent-variable identifiability）**，而是**统计检验的可辨识性**——即"这个检验统计量是否能唯一确定分布 P=Q"。三段论：

1. **Theorem 1（isotropic Gaussian Optimality）** verbatim：
> "The integrated square bias (ISB) over query points is given by [k-NN / kernel 两式]... and among distributions with a scalar-based covariance constraint, **the isotropic Gaussian is the unique minimizer of the integrated square bias.**"

   ⚠️ 注意 "among distributions with a **scalar-based covariance constraint**" —— 最优性是**在给定协方差标量约束（trace/det/Frobenius/spectral radius 四种之一）的类内**成立的，不是无条件最优。目标函数是 Fisher information functional $J(p)$，通过下游 k-NN/kernel 探针的偏差项导出。

2. **Theorem 3（Insufficiency of K Moments）** verbatim：
> "Minimizing the following objective with $c_k>0,\forall k$ ... for finite $K$ does not imply $P_\theta^{(a)} = Q^{(a)}$"

3. **"The Stability and Identifiability Conundrum"** verbatim：
> "...with $J_{f_\theta}(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{K\times P}$ the Jacobian matrix–hereby creating an impractical situation where **training stability and identifiability can not be achieved simultaneously.**"

   这是拒绝矩匹配（Jarque-Bera 等）的理由，转而用 Epps-Pulley 特征函数检验：§4.2.3 标题即 "Characteristic Functions are Stable, Scalable and **Identifiable**"，依据 **Theorem 10 (Cramér-Wold)**：$X \overset{d}{=} Y \iff \langle X,a\rangle \overset{d}{=} \langle Y,a\rangle, \forall a$ —— 一维投影族足以确定高维分布，这才是 "sketching" 合法的根据。**Theorem 2（Sufficiency of directional tests）** 给出 level + power 的渐近保证。

**对你的 C(z,a) 反事实共推断的意义**：LeJEPA 的理论**完全不涉及动作、不涉及转移算子**。它是纯 encoder 端的边缘分布几何论断（embedding 分布应为 isotropic Gaussian），对"$z$ 是否编码了足以让 $C(z,a)$ 良定义且对 $a$ 敏感的信息"**零覆盖**。这是一个真实的理论缺口，而不是我的推断——见下面 2607.22430。

## 四、⭐ 为什么"latent 优于 pixel"—— 理论与实证证据

这是任务书里最值得回答的一问。分四层，可信度递减：

### 4.1 最强的理论：Fisher/子空间错配（非 JEPA 论文）
**2402.11337** *Learning by Reconstruction Produces Uninformative Features For Perception* | 2024/02/17 | **Balestriero & LeCun** | Comments *(空)* | **VERIFIED**
> 摘要 verbatim: "...the former allocates a model's capacity towards a subspace of the data explaining the observed variance—**a subspace with uninformative features for the latter**. For example, the supervised TinyImagenet task with images **projected onto the top subspace explaining 90% of the pixel variance can be solved with 45% test accuracy. Using the bottom subspace instead, accounting for only 20% of the pixel variance, reaches 55%**."

这才是"pixel 重建劣于 latent 预测"的**机制性根据**：重建损失按像素方差分配容量，而感知有用信息在低方差子空间。

**2407.03475** *How JEPA Avoids Noisy Features: The Implicit Bias of Deep Linear Self Distillation Networks* | 2024/07/03 | **Apple**（Littwin, Saremi, Nakkiran, Susskind） | Comments: `Technical report` | **VERIFIED**

## 五、⭐⭐ 与你的 C(z,a) 反事实共推断最直接相关的一组

这是我认为对你的选题**最有价值的发现**，独立四条证据线指向同一结论：**JEPA 世界模型在 on-policy 预测上很准，但在反事实动作查询上系统性失效**。

### 5.1 理论（最重要）
**2607.22430** *On the Identifiability of Controlled World Models* | v1 2026/07/24 | **清华 (Zhang Xiangteng, Guan Yang, Zhang Ya-Qin, Li Shengbo Eben)** | Comments *(空)* | **VERIFIED（已通读 HTML）**

摘要 verbatim：
> "Recently, LeCun's JEPA provides a compelling framework... but leaves a fundamental question: **can it recover the controlled dynamics from nonlinear observations?** This paper presents a joint identifiability condition... which consists of two coupled components: (1) **representation identifiability** and (2) **transition identifiability**."

设定（Assumption 1–2）：$z_t \sim \mathcal N(0,I_d)$，$z_{t+1}=Az_t+Ba_t+\xi_t$，学习者为 encoder $h$ + 确定性 action-conditioned predictor $F$。

**Theorem 3（Counterfactual error amplification）verbatim**：
> "For every $\delta>0$, the predictor class contains a continuous predictor $F_\delta$ satisfying $\mathcal E_\pi(F_\delta)=\delta,\quad \mathcal E_{\rm cf}(F_\delta)=\frac{\delta}{\rho_{\rm tr}(\pi)}$."
> "The theorem shows that **exact transition identifiability does not imply well-conditioned approximate identification.** ... when $\rho_{\rm tr}(\pi)$ is small, the behavior policy provides little variation along the least-excited action direction, and **the training objective weakly penalizes errors in the corresponding action response.**"

Conclusion verbatim：
> "In contrast, **state-only prediction captures the behavior-policy-averaged future and does not generally identify how alternative actions affect the next state.** We further derive quantitative identifiability bounds and show that **weak conditional action excitation can make transition identification poorly conditioned, allowing accurate on-policy prediction to coexist with substantially larger counterfactual error.**"

自述局限 verbatim：
> "Our analysis is **limited to invertible observations, linear Gaussian latent dynamics, an exact representation constraint, and identification of the controlled conditional mean.** Future work should extend these guarantees to **finite-sample settings and to nonlinear or partially observed dynamics.**"

👉 **这篇几乎就是你 C(z,a) 选题的理论前置**。它把"$z$ 可辨识"与"$C(\cdot,a)$ 可辨识"明确拆成两件事，并给出 $\rho_{\rm tr}(\pi)$（conditional action excitation）作为条件数。它留下的口子正是你的机会：**非线性动力学、有限样本、部分可观测**。

**2606.12471** *Identifiability Without Gaussianity: Symbolic World Models and Near-Infinite Temporal Consistency*（2026/06/09）—— 直接松弛上文的高斯假设，**VERIFIED 存在**。
