### 9. Latent Action Learning Requires Supervision in the Presence of Distractors  **VERIFIED**
- arXiv: **2502.00379**（用户给的 id 正确）| https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00379
- dateline：`[Submitted on 1 Feb 2025 (v1), last revised 12 Jun 2025 (this version, v5)]`

### 10. What Do Latent Action Models Actually Learn?  **VERIFIED**
- arXiv: **2506.15691** | dateline `[Submitted on 27 May 2025 (v1), last revised 12 Nov 2025 (this version, v3)]`
- **Comments 原文**：`Accepted by NeurIPS-25`
- 机构：Microsoft Research（Chuheng Zhang, Tim Pearce, Pushi Zhang, Kaixin Wang, Li Zhao, Jiang Bian）+ Tsinghua
- 一句话：线性 LAM ≡ 对「可控变化 q + 外生噪声 ε」做 PCA。
- **这是本领域最接近「理论/界」的结果**（原文）：
  - `Proposition 4.1 (Linear LAM is PCA)`：`ℒ = 𝔼[‖(BD−I)(q+ε)‖²₂]`
  - `Proposition 4.2 (Linear LAM tries to capture q+ε)`：最优解 `B = U_{d_z}`（Σ_{q+ε} 的 top-d_z 主成分），`The minimum loss is given by the sum of the eigenvalues of ... corresponding to the discarded principal components ℒ* = Σ_{i=d_z+1}^{d_o} λ_i.`
  - 关键分界：`When d_z ≥ d_a, the capacity of the latent is large enough to capture all the information about the controllable change q, the minimum loss ℒ* = 0`。
  - 即：**压缩上限的判据是 latent 维度 d_z 与真实动作维度 d_a 的比较，而不是与观测维度比较；低于 d_a 就必然丢失，丢失量正好是被丢弃主成分特征值之和。**但注意这只在 ε=0 且 q=Xa 线性情形成立。
  - 另一条被作者称为「文献里此前未记录」的危险：`a lack of randomization in a data collection policy can harm the latents learned by LAM`（专家数据本身就会毁掉 latent）。

### 17. What Matters for Latent Actions in Robot Learning  **VERIFIED** ★这就是用户找的那篇
- arXiv: **2608.19613** | https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19613 | dateline `[Submitted on 20 Aug 2026]`
- **Comments 原文**：`Project page: this https URL`（无 venue）
- Subjects: cs.RO; cs.CV
- 机构（HTML thanks 块）：复旦大学智能机器人与先进制造学院 / 清华深研院 / 深圳理工大学（Xizhou Bu, Qingda Hu, Lei Zhou … Xiaoshuai Hao，16 作者）
- 一句话：首个 LAM 系统性实证研究——统一到同一 autoencoding 框架，扫 **41 个设计选项**（建模范式 / 目标与正则 / 注入策略）+ **4 个 proxy metric**，跨 3 个 benchmark。
- **对本课题最重要的两条结论（原文）**：
  1. proxy metric 不可靠：`while the Pearson correlation is consistently high across settings, the Spearman correlation remains noticeably lower, indicating that the proxy metrics are more reliable for coarse-grained model screening than for fine-grained model ranking` / `they fail to consistently maintain a strict monotonic ordering across configurations and are therefore not suitable for identifying the optimal model.`
  2. **latent action 作为「接口」输给作为「预训练信号」**：DAP（latent action 只用于微调 VLM backbone、预测物理动作时丢弃）胜过 LAP（latent 当动作接口），原因是 `the physical action branch retains direct access to the complete VLM features, avoiding the information loss introduced by a strict latent bottleneck` 与 `LAP imposes a stronger information bottleneck, making its effectiveness dependent on whether the latent space can preserve sufficient information for accurate control.`
  3. 明确点名光流类 latent 会丢什么：`Such a representation may discard visual cues relevant to transition dynamics, including contact, occlusion and disocclusion, local deformation, and object boundary changes, which cannot always be adequately characterized by pixel-wise displacements.`
  4. 反直觉观察：`A possible explanation is that the discrete bottleneck encourages the model to capture reusable high-level action primitives rather than overfit to fine-grained continuous variations in the source domain.`（离散瓶颈的「丢失」有时是跨域正则）
  5. 连续正则的形式不重要：`For continuous latent actions, VAE, Sparsity, and SIGReg achieve comparable downstream performance … the underlying LAM is relatively insensitive to the specific form of continuous regularization, provided that the regularization strength is properly selected.`
